What Trexure does

Four jobs, done with restraint.

No blockchain in your books. No payroll leaking to the public ledger. Just a clean, verifiable receipt — every time.

01

Shielded by default

Every payment is ZK-shielded. The public ledger sees a proof hash — never sender, recipient, or amount.

02

Auto-reconciled

The on-chain leg and the fiat leg meet on one shared reference. We match them automatically — pending to settled.

03

Stripe-style receipts

FX, fees, slippage, both references, and a privacy block — in one JSON object that pipes straight into your accounting.

04

Your view key, your control

You hold the key; we decrypt server-side on demand. The key is never returned, never logged — audit-trail included.

The lifecycle

One payment, four states.

From an opaque shield on the public ledger to a settled receipt your accountant loves — the path is the same every time.

1
The shield

Public ledger view

A competitor scraping Stellar sees only a proof hash and a “Shielded” badge. No payroll leaks.

2
The decrypt

Apply view key

Your key unwraps the payload server-side. Sender, recipient, and amount appear — for your eyes only.

3
The match

Auto-reconcile

A signed fiat webhook arrives. We stitch the on-chain hash to the bank reference on a shared intent id.

4
The output

Unified receipt

A clean, Stripe-ified receipt — copy JSON, export PDF. The blockchain is completely abstracted away.

Batch payments

Pay a whole roster, privately.

Disburse to up to 50 receivers in one shielded batch. Each payout becomes a private, claimable note — no public payroll, no spreadsheet of wallet addresses.

01

Build the roster

Up to 50 rows: an amount, a label like “Alice · invoice #42”, and an optional email. Compose once, send once.

02

Minted as shielded notes

Each row mints a private claimable note. The ledger stores only a commitment; receiver details stay encrypted under your view key.

03

Hand out claim links

Copy a note or claim link per receiver. They claim to a Stellar wallet or a PH bank account, on their terms.

04

Live roll-up

Every disbursement moves send → on-chain → reconcile → receipt, with claimed, unclaimed, and failed counts per batch.

Employee payroll

Run payroll, not just payouts.

Beyond one-off disbursements: onboard your team, let them access earned wages early, and turn non-monetary benefits into cash — all private, all reconciled, settled on the same rail.

01

Onboard the package

Set up an employee with a base salary and benefits — monetary or non-monetary. Package changes are effective-dated, with full auditable history kept.

02

Earned-wage advance

Employees draw against earned-but-unpaid salary. Trexure shows the eligible-now figure and pays out on the private rail — fee and per-cycle cap enforced.

03

Auto-netted at payroll

When salary runs, outstanding advances net out first-in-first-out and mark repaid — no spreadsheets, and guarded against concurrent double-settle.

04

Benefit-to-cash conversion

Convert the convertible portion of a non-monetary benefit into withdrawable cash, disbursed on the same rail — with a per-tenant rate and fee.

Reports

finance team asks for.

Five report types, drawn straight from the receipts you already hold — so the numbers never drift. CSV for the ledger, PDF for the file, JSON for the pipeline.

Reconciliation Statement

Every settled payment for the period, plus an exceptions list finance chases: in-flight, failed, and funds-in-custody. Totals by currency.

The report only Trexure can produce: shielded payments decrypted under your view key, with on-chain and fiat proof — and its own audit trail. For an auditor, the BIR, or AMLC.
