The problem

On a public ledger, privacy is all-or-nothing.

A Stellar payment is either fully public — any competitor scraping the ledger sees sender, recipient, and amount — or, once routed through a ZK privacy pool, opaque to everyone, including the paying company's own finance team. But finance still has to prove "on-chain hash X produced bank deposit Y" for AML/KYC and bookkeeping. Existing privacy tooling proves a payment happened; it doesn't reconcile that proof against the real fiat leg or turn it into something an accountant can use. Trexure closes that gap.

No hand-waving

What's real vs. what's mocked.

This is a hackathon build with a deliberate boundary: the cryptography and the on-chain settlement are genuine; the fiat provider is a faithful stand-in that fires the exact signed webhook a real anchor would.

REAL ZK verification
Groth16 proofs are verified on-chain on Stellar testnet via a Soroban BLS12-381 multi-pairing check — not a mock.
REAL On-chain leg
Payments submit real Soroban transactions; a worker watches the contract event and confirms them.
REAL Shield · decrypt · reconcile
View-key decryption, intent-based reconciliation, and the Stripe-shaped receipt all run for real.
MOCK Fiat anchor
A built-in Mock Anchor stands in for a real provider (e.g. Xendit), firing the same HMAC-signed webhook so it swaps in later without changing the contract.
