# Trexure Pitch Deck — Full-Slide Image Prompts

Image-generation prompts for the two full-slide visual hooks in
[`pitch-deck-draft-Trexure.pptx`](pitch-deck-draft-Trexure.pptx). Each number
matches a `[FULL-SLIDE IMAGE — see image prompt #N]` placeholder in the deck.
Paste a prompt into an image model, then drop the result behind the matching
slide (title/tagline text on #1, headline + bullets on #2).

---

## Prompt #1 — Title / hook slide (Slide 1)

A cinematic, wide 16:9 hero image for a fintech-meets-crypto product called
"Trexure." Central concept: a single glowing payment/coin transforming into a
clean, tidy financial receipt as it passes through a translucent shield. Left
side shows chaotic, exposed streams of transaction data (numbers, names)
scattered on a dark public ledger; right side shows the same data resolving into
an elegant, ordered receipt. Deep plum/aubergine background with warm gold
accents, subtle circuit-and-ledger motifs, soft volumetric light, premium and
trustworthy mood, minimal, lots of negative space in the center-left for a title
overlay. Modern enterprise-SaaS aesthetic, high detail, no text, no logos.

---

## Prompt #2 — Problem slide (Slide 2)

A moody, 16:9 conceptual image conveying financial privacy exposure on a public
blockchain. A large transparent glass ledger wall in a dark server-room-like
space, with rows of sensitive payroll entries — salaries, names, amounts —
glowing and fully readable to anonymous silhouetted figures watching from the
shadows. A sense of surveillance and vulnerability; cold blue-teal lighting with
a single stark spotlight, faint gold Stellar-like particle streams flowing
across the ledger. Cinematic, slightly ominous, high contrast, generous darker
space in the upper-left for a headline overlay. Photorealistic-conceptual style,
no text, no logos.
