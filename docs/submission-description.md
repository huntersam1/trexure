# Trexure — private, provable payments for real businesses on Stellar

Every public blockchain carries an uncomfortable truth for businesses: putting payroll, vendor payments, or contractor payouts on-chain broadcasts salaries, counterparties, and cash positions to competitors and the entire world. That radical transparency is precisely why enterprises still can't move real money on-chain — and why crypto payroll has stayed a niche curiosity rather than a serious tool. Trexure closes that gap. It lets a company send **confidential payments on Stellar** — amounts and the sender↔recipient link shielded with zero-knowledge cryptography — while keeping every payment **fully provable to the people who matter**: an auditor or regulator can be handed a verifiable, on-chain-anchored receipt for a single payment without ever exposing the rest of the books. *Private to the world, provable to your auditor.* This is the missing primitive that turns Stellar from a fast settlement network into a viable rail for real corporate treasuries — payroll, batch disbursements, earned-wage advances, and convertible benefits. It complements Stellar's own push toward confidential assets by adding the productized workflow layer on top: employee onboarding, reconciliation into clean receipts, and compliance-grade reporting — the things a finance team actually needs before it will trust a chain with its salaries.

**For reviewers:** Trexure runs live on Stellar testnet — real zero-knowledge proof verification and real on-chain transactions, not a simulation. The only mocked component is the fiat off-ramp, which fires the exact same signed webhook a production anchor would, so everything downstream is genuine. We suggest starting with the guided demo: sign in as the seeded admin, send a shielded payroll payout, watch it reconcile into a Stripe-style receipt, then open the shareable disclosure link as a logged-out "auditor" and click **Verify on-chain** to watch the proof check against Stellar in real time. From there, explore the HR suite — onboard an employee with a compensation package, run an earned-wage salary advance, or convert a non-monetary benefit into a withdrawable payout. Note that the on-chain private-value payout features are gated behind a feature flag and a funded testnet key; if a payout step is disabled in the environment you're viewing, the rest of the flow still demonstrates the privacy-and-proof model end to end. We'd genuinely welcome your feedback on the problem framing as much as on the execution — we believe confidential, auditable payments are the unlock Stellar needs to win enterprise treasury, and we'd love to hear whether you agree.
