# Trexure — Pitch Deck Image Prompts

Full-slide visual hooks for `pitch-deck-draft-Trexure.pptx`. Paste each prompt into an image model. Numbers match the `[FULL-SLIDE IMAGE — see image prompt #N]` placeholders in the deck.

---

## Image Prompt #1 — Slide 1 (Title / Hook)

A cinematic, full-bleed hero image for a fintech privacy product called Trexure. A single glowing golden envelope or receipt emerging from a dark, dissolving cloud of encrypted data — streams of faint hexadecimal characters and blurred blockchain nodes fading into darkness behind it, while the receipt in front is crisp, clean, and legible. Deep aubergine-black background with warm gold accent lighting. Mood: premium, trustworthy, quietly powerful — the moment chaos resolves into clarity. Style: modern editorial 3D render with soft depth-of-field, subtle grain, high contrast. Leave the upper-center and lower-center areas relatively dark and uncluttered so overlaid white title text remains readable. 16:9, no text in the image.

## Image Prompt #2 — Slide 2 (Problem)

A full-bleed split-screen conceptual illustration contrasting two states of a crypto payment. Left half: a bright, exposed public ledger — rows of legible payroll entries (names, salary amounts, dates) glowing on a cold blue transparent glass panel, with faint watching eyes and magnifying glasses hovering over it, conveying exposure and surveillance. Right half: the same payment collapsed into a single opaque, sealed dark vault or shielded blob marked only with one abstract proof-hash glyph — private but with a broken, dangling thread where the "proof of what became what" is missing. A subtle frayed or disconnected link runs across the center seam between the two halves. Mood: tension, an impossible either/or choice. Style: clean modern vector-meets-3D editorial illustration, restrained palette of cold blue (left) and deep aubergine/charcoal (right) with a single gold accent on the broken link. Keep the top strip darker/simpler for an overlaid headline. 16:9, no readable real text beyond abstract glyphs.
